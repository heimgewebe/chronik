# Operator ecosystem alignment

Chronik is the append-only event ledger and historical evidence axis of the Heimgewebe operator ecosystem.

- Plexer can deliver bounded operational events into Chronik.
- Grabowski may export task-local agent-run events only through explicit/manual paths.
- Bureau owns tasks and claims; Chronik records events, not commitments.
- Leitstand may read and display Chronik state, but Chronik does not push UI decisions.
- Heimlern may learn from historical outcomes and propose adjustments; it does not silently apply policy.

Chronik must stay useful when Plexer, Leitstand, Bureau, Grabowski or heimlern are unavailable. The ledger is not the lever.
